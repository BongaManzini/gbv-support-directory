Emergency Safety Planning (Before Leaving)

If you are planning to leave an unsafe environment, keeping critical essentials packed in a safe place with a trusted friend can protect your future independence:

1. Essential Documents

ID Books, Birth Certificates, Clinic immunization cards, SASSA cards, Passports, Bank cards.

2. Medication & Essentials

Chronic prescription meds, spare phone charger, house keys, emergency cash.

3. Safe Transportation

Identify the nearest police station or Thuthuzela Care Centre (TCC) which provide 24/7 guarded shelter.
